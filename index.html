Solid Sun/Moon-coloured lines point towards each rising or setting body; dashed teal lines point in the opposite direction towards possible photographers. Camera bearings are 180° opposite the celestial bearings. These are azimuth-only guides, not a guarantee of vertical alignment or an unobstructed photograph.

5 · Sun, Moon & galactic core

Two rays per body mark today's rise and set azimuths; a heavier ray shows the current azimuth (dashed below the horizon). Lines follow the visible ground/3D mesh. Astronomical horizon, not local obstructions.

Each row has a fixed sky colour, with no blending. Civil, blue and golden periods overlap, so short transition bands identify the civil and sunrise/sunset rows. Click times to jump there. Event boundaries remain unchanged (blue −6° to −4°, golden −4° to +6°). Local UTC offset and flat astronomical horizon apply.

Arcs represent celestial directions through the selected local day on a schematic sky shell. Dashed segments are below the astronomical horizon.

Azimuth is clockwise from true north; altitude is relative to the astronomical horizon. UTC offset is explicit; change it for daylight saving or another region.
